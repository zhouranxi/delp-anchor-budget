\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item A pre-registered 160-run test transfers an own-segment bias
repair from MLP to convolutional active-dendrite networks.
\item The repair collapses on conv backbones: 4 of 12 dose cells
significant, and final accuracy significantly reverses.
\item The dead-neuron artifact vanishes because conv+GroupNorm
mortality is exactly zero, not because the instrument improved.
\item A GroupNorm-in-MLP control rejects the dying-gate hypothesis:
mortality rises while the repair gain collapses.
\item Repair gain tracks baseline weakness; strong-feature backbones
silently trade retention for learning.
\end{itemize}
